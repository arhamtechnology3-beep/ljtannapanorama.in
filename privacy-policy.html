1. Introduction & Commitment

At Codename Majha Ghar ("we", "our", or "us"), we value your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal information collected when you explore our real estate project, request pricing, schedule site visits, or contact our sales executives.

2. Information We Collect

We only collect essential information required to coordinate project details and site visit arrangements:

  • Full Name: To address you personally in communications.
  • 10-Digit Mobile Phone Number: To send verified project brochures, rate cards, floor plans, and site visit passes via Phone Call or WhatsApp.

Your information is used strictly for legitimate project marketing:

We strictly adhere to a zero-spam policy. We do NOT sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third-party telemarketers.

4. Data Security

We implement standard SSL encryption, firewalls, and restricted administrative access to prevent unauthorized disclosure, misuse, or tampering with your personal contact numbers.
